## Slide 03: BAU Priority: Digital OTP Verification & 510-Char Pending Revamp
**Subtitle**: Eliminating Signature Pending Discrepancies and Clarifying Agent Communication Channels  
**Tagline**: Replacing error-prone ink signature verification with instant SMS/email OTP while radically upgrading pending message clarity.

### Key Operational Metrics
| Metric | Value | Operational Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pending Message Limit** | `510 Chars` | Expanded from 70 (EN) / 31 (ZH) characters (+628%) |
| **Language Format** | `Dual EN/ZH` | Separate 510-character buffers for English and Chinese |
| **Signature Pendings** | `-60% Est.` | OTP removes ink verification discrepancies |
| **Adoption Expansion** | `2027 Target`| Active review with Legal & Compliance in progress |

### Detailed Pillars
- **iChange OTP Authentication Enablement**:
  - Replaces manual signature verification with secure One-Time Password (OTP) verification on designated servicing request types.
  - Slashes signature discrepancy pending cases, avoids customer branch recall visits, and accelerates straight-through turnaround.
  - Currently undergoing rigorous review with Legal & Compliance to expand approved servicing request types throughout 2027.
- **Comprehensive Pending Message Revamp**:
  - Expands message limits from legacy 70 (EN) / 31 (ZH) characters to 510 separate characters for English and Chinese.
  - Eliminates cryptic legacy abbreviations, split pending notices, duplicate messages, and obsolete alphanumeric codes.
  - Embeds clear, dual-language comprehensive requirement descriptions that agents and policyholders understand on first read.

# Section 3: Lessons Learned & Innovation Sharing

## Slide 13: Lessons Learned 1: Witness Signature Streamlining & POS Tips
**Subtitle**: Removing Unnecessary Administrative Friction and Establishing a Proactive Agency Communication Channel  
**Tagline**: Converting operational friction into permanent workflow simplifications and practical submission guidance for agents.

### Operational Achievements
- **Streamlined Witness Signature Requirement**: Removed unnecessary administrative hurdles while strictly maintaining governance, anti-fraud, and compliance standards. Slashed rejected submissions and branch recall visits.
- **Bi-Monthly POS Tips Launch**: Established a regular communication channel converting operational pain points and case observations into practical guidance for agents, driving a +30% increase in First-Time-Right (FTR) submissions.

---

## Slide 14: Lessons Learned 2: Product "SelectWise" In-Line Declaration
**Subtitle**: Embedding Customer Declarations for Existing Exclusions and Loadings Directly at Point of Submission  
**Tagline**: Transforming plan-change documentation to capture upfront customer acknowledgement, cutting pending rounds to zero.

### Operational Achievements
- **Point-of-Submission Declaration**: Customers acknowledge applicable existing exclusions and premium loadings directly in the primary plan-change documentation.
- **Elimination of Follow-Up Chasing**: Completely eradicated the need to issue separate pending requests for supplementary customer confirmation, shortening case touch time by 35%.

## Slide 15: Lessons Learned 3: Change of Ownership Digital Leap (iVerify & POSA)
**Subtitle**: Integrating iChange with iVerify and Pre-Populating Customer Data Directly into POSA  
**Tagline**: Connecting customer identity verification with internal back-office servicing to eliminate manual re-keying and human errors.

### Operational Achievements
- **iChange + iVerify Integration**: Seamless digital link between alteration requests and cryptographic biometric identity verification.
- **Automated POSA Pre-Population**: Customer-entered data flows automatically into the internal servicing workbench, reducing manual staff data re-entry by 80% and guaranteeing 100% cross-system consistency.

## Slide 16: Lessons Learned 4: Mandatory e-Advice Rollout & ESG Impact
**Subtitle**: Transitioning Eligible Communications to Digital Channels: AIA+, Email, and Mobile SMS  
**Tagline**: Driving operational sustainability and substantial cost savings by replacing paper notices with secure digital delivery.

### Operational Achievements
- **Paperless Servicing**: Transitioned high-volume annual statements and billing notices from paper post to AIA+, email, and mobile SMS.
- **Cost Reduction & ESG Goals**: Substantial recurrent savings in secure printing, paper consumption, and postal tariffs, advancing AIA corporate carbon reduction objectives with robust exception handling for vulnerable customers.
