Digital Authentication
iChange OTP Verification Enablement

Transitioning customer request authentication from manual signature verification to secure One-Time Password (OTP) verification.

  • Replaces error-prone signature verification with SMS/email OTP authentication on designated request types.
  • Drastically lowers signature discrepancy pending cases and accelerates customer straight-through turnaround.
  • Currently under active review with Legal & Compliance to expand eligible request types across 2027.
Communication Clarity
Comprehensive Pending Message Revamp

Expanding message character limits from legacy 70 (EN) / 31 (ZH) characters to 510 separate characters for English and Chinese.

  • Eliminates cryptic abbreviations, split pending notices, duplicate messages, and obsolete alphanumeric codes.
  • Embeds clear, dual-language comprehensive requirement descriptions that agents understand on first read.
  • Directly reduces agent follow-up enquiries and back-and-forth clarifications, cutting case touch time.
STREAMLINED CUSTOMER REQUEST JOURNEY WITH OTP VERIFICATION
[01] Request Intake: Customer initiates alteration via iChange or AIA+ portal (Instant)
[02] Dual-Factor OTP Auth: SMS/Email OTP dispatched; verified instantly without signature queue (< 30 sec)
[03] Automated Validation: POS rule engine validates request against policy ledger (Automated)
[04] Clear Pending Dispatch: If exception occurs, 510-character dual-language explicit guidance dispatched immediately
Speaker Notes: Signature discrepancies represent one of our largest historical pending drivers. With OTP verification in iChange and 510-character clear dual-language pending messages, we cut rework at the root.
